Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 November 2026 · Version 2026-11-27

Reality Health Bridge is the stock, sales and price book used by pharmacies, drug shops and clinics. This policy explains, in plain words, what information the app keeps, why, where it is stored, who can see it, how long it is kept, and the rights you have. It follows Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 and its Regulations.

1. Who we are

Reality Health Bridge is made and run by Reality Health Ltd, trading as REALITY HEALTH SYSTEM ("RHS", "we", "us"), a company registered in Uganda. Our contact details are at the end of this policy.

2. Who is responsible for which data

  • Your facility's records (stock, sales, patients, staff): the facility is in charge of these records and decides what is entered. RHS stores and processes them only to provide the app to the facility, on the facility's behalf. In the words of the law, the facility is the data controller and RHS is its data processor.
  • Account and business information (who registered, how to reach you, subscriptions, payments, feedback): RHS is responsible for these, as the data controller.

3. What we collect

About the facilityName, location, phone, email, slogan, logo, digital stamp, and operating licences or certificates you upload.
About the people who use the appFull name, phone, email (optional), position and cadre, username, and role (super admin, admin or staff). Passwords are never stored in readable form: only a one-way scrambled version that cannot be turned back into the password.
About patients and customersOnly what your staff type in on a sale: name, phone, diagnosis and notes (all optional), plus the patient number, the medicines given (with batch and expiry), amounts paid and balances. See section 5.
Stock and moneyProducts, batches, expiry dates, suppliers, prices, sales, payments and stock adjustments.
Subscription paymentsThe Mobile Money number used, amount, plan, and the transaction references from our payment provider and the mobile network.
DevicesA random device number, a label (for example "Android phone"), the app version, when the device last connected, and whether it has records waiting to send. We do not collect location, contacts, photos or anything else from the phone.
Messages to usFeedback, star ratings and support requests you send from the app, with your name, role and facility.
Security recordsLogins, failed login attempts and the internet address they came from, and a log of important actions, so we can stop misuse and investigate problems.

4. Why we use it

  • To run the app for your facility: save your records, keep all your devices in step, and let you work offline.
  • To set up your account, check who is logging in, and keep each facility's records apart.
  • To manage your free trial and subscription and to confirm payments.
  • To support you: answer questions, fix problems and restore records when asked.
  • To send you important notices about your account, the app and changes to these terms.
  • To improve the app, using feedback and counts (for example how many facilities use a feature). We never use patient information for this.
  • To protect the app and its users, and to meet our duties under Ugandan law.

We use your information because you asked us to provide the app (our agreement with you, the Terms of Use), because you agreed to this policy, and where the law requires us to.

We never sell information, and we never use it for advertising.

5. Patient information

Health information is "special personal data" under Ugandan law and needs extra care. So:

  • Patient fields are optional. Facilities should record only what they need to serve the patient and to meet National Drug Authority and other record-keeping rules.
  • The facility must tell patients that their details are recorded and why, and must have a lawful reason to record them (for example, providing medicines and care, or the patient's consent).
  • Only the facility's own logins can see its patients. RHS staff look at a facility's records only when the facility asks us for help, to fix a fault, or when the law requires it.
  • If a patient asks to see, correct or delete their details, they should ask the facility. The facility can change or delete a sale in the app, and we will help the facility when asked.

6. Who we share it with

We share only what each one needs to do its job for us, under a duty to keep it confidential:

  • Our hosting provider (AfriRegister, in Uganda), whose servers hold the app and its database.
  • Our payment provider (Nylon Pay) and the mobile networks (MTN, Airtel), only for subscription payments: your Mobile Money number, the amount and the reference.
  • Email services used to send notices to RHS staff and to you (for example, when a facility registers). These emails contain facility and contact details, never patient information, and may pass through servers outside Uganda (for example Google's).
  • Google Drive, only if you choose to save a backup there. The backup goes into your own Google account, and the app can see only the files it saved there.
  • Authorities, only when Ugandan law or a court order requires it. Where the law allows, we will tell the facility first.

If Reality Health Ltd is ever reorganised or sold, records would move to the new owner only under this same policy, and we would tell you first.

7. Where it is stored

Your records are kept in two places: on each phone or computer your facility uses (so the app works offline), and on our server, which is hosted in Uganda. Records travel between them over a secure, encrypted (https) connection.

Records on your own devices are your facility's responsibility: lock your phones and computers, and remove a device from the app (System → Devices) if it is lost or given away.

8. How we protect it

  • Encrypted connections (https) between the app and our server.
  • Passwords stored only in scrambled form; each device gets its own key, which can be withdrawn.
  • Each facility can only ever reach its own records; the server checks this on every request.
  • Super admins decide what other admins can see; staff cannot see profit figures, reports or the facility's settings.
  • Repeated wrong passwords are slowed down and blocked.
  • Only a small number of RHS staff can reach the admin system, each with their own login, and their important actions are logged.
  • Regular backups of the server.

No system is perfectly safe, but we work to keep your information secure and review our measures regularly.

9. How long we keep it

  • While your facility uses the app, we keep its records so the app works on all your devices.
  • 12 months after your subscription or free trial ends, so you can come back without losing anything. After that we permanently delete the facility's records from our server. Copies in our server backups are replaced within 3 months after that.
  • If you ask us to delete your facility's data, we do it within 30 days of confirming the request with you (see section 10).
  • Records of payments to RHS are kept for as long as Uganda's tax and accounting laws require, then deleted.
  • Security logs are kept for up to 2 years.

Records stay on your own devices until you delete them there or uninstall the app.

10. Your rights

Under the Data Protection and Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • Know and see what information we hold about you, and get a copy. Your facility can download a full backup any time (System → Backup).
  • Correct information that is wrong. Most of it can be changed in the app (Settings).
  • Ask us to delete it. The facility's super admin can ask in the app (Settings → Privacy → Delete your facility's data), or contact us. We will confirm with you before deleting.
  • Object to how we use your information, or ask us to stop using it for a purpose.
  • Complain to the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO) of Uganda if you are not happy with how we handled your information. We would like the chance to put it right first, so please contact us.

We answer requests within 30 days. We may need to check that the request really comes from you or your facility.

11. If something goes wrong

If information is lost, stolen or seen by someone who should not see it, we will act at once to stop it, tell the Personal Data Protection Office as the law requires, and tell the facilities affected, with what happened and what they should do.

12. Children

The app is for health facilities and their staff, not for children. A facility may record medicines given to a child patient; the facility is responsible for having the parent's or guardian's agreement where the law requires it.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the change matters to how your information is used, the app will show it and ask your facility's super admin to agree again.
