1. The service

The app helps pharmacies, drug shops, clinics and other health facilities record stock (with batch and expiry), sales and payments, print receipts, see expiry warnings and money reports, give logins to their team, and use all of this on several phones and computers, also without internet. We may improve, add or change features over time.

2. Accounts and logins

  • The person who registers the facility becomes its super admin. They control the facility profile, create logins for admins and staff, and choose what each admin may see.
  • Give us true details when you register, and keep them up to date.
  • Every person should have their own login. Keep passwords secret. The facility is responsible for what is done with its logins.
  • Tell us at once if you think someone got into your facility's account, and remove lost or stolen devices (System → Devices).

3. Your responsibilities

  • Use the app only for a lawful health facility, and keep the licences and permits your work requires (for example from the National Drug Authority).
  • You decide what is recorded and you are responsible for it being correct. Check the stock, prices and totals the app shows against your own counts.
  • Record only the patient details you need, tell patients their details are recorded, and follow the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 for your patients' information (see the Privacy Policy).
  • Make sure your staff use the app properly and keep patient information confidential.

4. Not medical advice

The app is a record-keeping tool. It does not give medical or pharmaceutical advice, and it does not check prescriptions, doses or drug interactions. Decisions about patients and medicines stay with your qualified staff.

5. Free trial

A new facility gets a free trial of every feature (currently 14 days), with no payment needed to start. RHS may extend a trial. One free trial per facility.

6. Plans and payment

  • After the trial, the facility picks a plan. Plans differ in how many phones and computers can be used. The prices, the one-time setup fee and the monthly fee are shown in the app (System → Subscription) and on our website before you pay.
  • You can pay in the app with MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money, or pay RHS another way and receive an activation code.
  • Payments are not refundable once a plan is switched on, unless we fail to provide the service you paid for, or Ugandan law requires a refund. If you were charged by mistake, contact us and we will put it right.
  • We may change prices. A new price applies from your next renewal, and we will tell you in the app at least 30 days before.

7. When a subscription ends

The app warns you during the last days of your trial or subscription. After it ends, you keep full access for 7 more days with a reminder. After that the app locks until you renew. Your records are not deleted when the app locks; they are kept as explained in the Privacy Policy (12 months after the subscription ends).

8. Your records and backups

  • Your facility's records belong to your facility. You can download a full backup at any time (System → Backup).
  • Records entered without internet are saved on that device and sent to the server when it is online again. Until then they exist only on that device, so connect regularly, and keep backups.
  • We back up our server, but you should also keep your own backups.

9. Availability and support

We work to keep the app and our server running at all times, but we cannot promise there will never be a break, for example during maintenance, internet or power failures, or problems with our providers. The app keeps working on your devices when our server cannot be reached. Support is by phone, WhatsApp and email (below).

10. Fair use

Do not:

  • use the app for anything illegal, including selling medicines you are not allowed to sell;
  • try to reach another facility's records, break the app's security, or overload our server;
  • copy, resell or rent out the app, or share one facility's subscription with other businesses (branches of the same facility may share one account);
  • enter false information or someone else's details without the right to.

11. Suspension

We may suspend a facility's use of the app if these terms are seriously broken, if we must to protect other users or the app, or if the law requires it. Unless it is urgent, we will contact the facility first and give it a chance to put things right.

12. Leaving

You can stop using the app at any time; simply do not renew. Download a backup first if you want to keep your records. The facility's super admin can also ask us to delete the facility's data (Settings → Privacy). Deletion cannot be undone.
